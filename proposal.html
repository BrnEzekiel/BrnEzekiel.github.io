Proposal template

What you get when you ask me for a quote. Print or save as PDF to reuse.

1. Understanding

Two or three sentences proving I understand your goal, your data, and your constraint. If I can't write this, we talk more first.

2. Scope

Exactly what is included — task count, dataset size, deliverable formats — and what is explicitly out of scope.

3. Milestones

Work split into demoable chunks, each with an acceptance check. You never pay for a black box.

4. Timeline

Start date, milestone dates, and what I need from you by when. Annotation and eval work usually starts within a week.

5. Revisions

Two revision rounds included on every engagement. More available if scope grows.

6. Next step

Reply with a yes and a start date — or pick "Book a call" on the contact form and we'll scope it live.
