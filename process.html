Process, on one page

The same flow for evals, annotation, and builds.

1 · Brief

You describe the goal in your own words — a paragraph beats a spec doc at this stage.

2 · Scope

I return a short proposal: what's in, what's out, milestones, dates. See the proposal template.

3 · Build

Milestones with demos. Async updates on your channel; blockers raised the same day, never month-end.

4 · Review

You test against the acceptance checks. Two revision rounds included.

5 · Handover

Deliverables, a short usage note, and a recorded walkthrough where it helps. Support window agreed up front.
